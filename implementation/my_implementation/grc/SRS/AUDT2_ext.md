Services: 1. Commission Management: Manages commissions, commissioners,
and legal services. 2. Risk Assurance and Quality Management: Handles
risk management and quality assurance processes. 3. Internal Audit
System: Manages audit plans, engagements, surveys, and implementations.

Quality Management System, Internal Audit System.

# General requirements

**Preparation for risk based Annual Internal plan**

-   The system shall allow Internal Auditors (IA) to create, update, and
    maintain an **Audit Universe** covering all potential auditable
    entities (directorates, units, zones, processes, systems, projects

-   The system shall provide configurable **risk assessment criteria**,
    including but not limited to:

```{=html}
<!-- -->
```
-   Inherent risk

-   Control effectiveness

-   Financial exposure

-   Compliance risk

-   Operational impact

-   Reputational risk

**CIA Review and Management Consultation**

-   The system shall allow the CIA to **review, comment, approve, or
    return** the RBAIP for revision.

-   The system shall allow the CIA to submit the reviewed RBAIP to
    **Management** for review and comments.

-   The system shall allow Management to:

-   Review the RBAIP

-   Provide formal recommendations and comments

-   Indicate adoption or non-adoption of the plan

-   The system shall record Management decisions and comments with
    timestamps and user identification.

-   **Audit Committee Review and Approval**

-   The system shall allow the CIA to submit the Management-adopted
    RBAIP to the **Audit Committee**.

-   The system shall support Audit Committee actions, including:

-   Review

-   Deliberation

-   Request for improvement

-   Approval

-   If the Audit Committee recommends improvements, the system shall:

-   Notify the CIA

-   Allow revisions

-   Support resubmission to the Committee

-   The system shall prevent implementation unless **formal Audit
    Committee approval** is recorded.

-   Risk Analysis and Scoring Execution

-   The system shall allow Internal Auditors (IA) to perform risk
    assessments against approved Audit Universe items only.

-   The system shall require IA to:

-   Select applicable risk factors

-   Assign scores per factor

-   Provide qualitative justifications for each score

-   The system shall automatically calculate:

-   Weighted risk scores

-   Overall inherent risk rating
